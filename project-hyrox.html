HYROX Phoenix

Full coverage of the HYROX World Series of Fitness Racing at State Farm Stadium. A cinematic look inside the world's largest indoor fitness competition — capturing the grit, community, and spectacle of race day from the arena floor to the stands.

From the athlete warm-up tunnels to the roar of the crowd, every frame was built to tell the human story behind the competition. HYROX draws thousands of athletes from around the world to test themselves across eight stations of running and functional fitness — and Inside Narrative Media was there to document every moment.
