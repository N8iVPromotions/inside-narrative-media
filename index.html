About Me

JOSEPH GENNARO IS AN ATHLETIC CONTENT DIRECTOR SHAPING A NEW ERA OF SPORTS AND REAL-LIFE STORY TELLING.

BORN IN UGANDA, AND RAISED IN SAN DIEGO, JOSEPH BUILT HIS CAREER FROM THE GROUND UP, DRIVEN BY OBSESSION, CREATIVITY, AND A RELENTLESS DESIRE TO TELL STORIES THAT FEEL REAL. WHAT STARTED AS A COVID PROJECT IN HIGH SCHOOL WITH A CAMERA TURNED INTO A FULL-TIME GIG POST COLLEGE.

JOSEPH IS BEST KNOWN FOR CAPTURING THE LIFE OF FORMER NFL PLAYER JARELL CARTER AND CREATING CONTENT FOR COLLEGE ATHLETES, REVEALING THE HUMAN STORY UNDERNEATH. HIS WORK BLENDS SPORTS, CULTURE, AND CINEMATIC VISUALS WITH AUTHENTICITY.

WHETHER IT'S A SHORT DOCUMENTARY, BRAND SHOOT, OR FITNESS CHALLENGE, THE GOAL IS ALWAYS THE SAME: TO CREATE FILMS THAT RESONATE ON AND OFF THE FIELD.
