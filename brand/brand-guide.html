06 — Usage

Logo Do's & Don'ts

Do

Use on Brand Backgrounds

Always place the primary circular mark on Carbon (#0a0a0a) or Obsidian (#111111). These are the canonical brand backgrounds.

Don't

Don't Place on Busy Photos

Never drop the logo directly over video frames or busy photography without a dark scrim underneath. Legibility must always be maintained.

Do

Maintain Clear Space

Keep a minimum clear space equal to the height of the "I" in INSIDE around all logo variants at all times.

Don't

Don't Recolor the Logo

Never apply brand accent blues, gradients, or any other color to the logo mark. Use only White, Smoke (#e8e8e8), or Carbon (#0a0a0a).

Do

Use the Right Variant

Use the circle mark for hero, social, and cinematic contexts. Use the horizontal lockup for navigation and business documents.

Don't

Don't Stretch or Skew

Always scale the logo proportionally. Never distort, rotate (outside the designed animation context), or crop the circular mark.

Do

Use Clash Display for All Headings

All display text must use Clash Display. Fallback to Arial Black when the web font is unavailable. Never substitute with serif or script fonts.

Don't

Don't Use Caveat Outside the Logo

Caveat is reserved exclusively for the circular logo mark. It must not be used in any other UI context, headline, or body copy.

07 — Brand Voice

Tone & Voice

Cinematic

Everything is a frame. We treat words with the same intentionality we treat footage — purposeful, composed, nothing wasted.

Authentic

We don't oversell. The work speaks. Copy is honest, direct, and grounded — never hype for its own sake.

Athlete-First

The story is always about the athlete, not the camera. Copy centers the human, the journey, the grit behind the achievement.

Precise

Short sentences. Confident statements. We avoid filler. Every word earns its place — just like every shot earns its place in the edit.
